# Preface

*Written in Chinese by River. Translated into English by ChatGPT.*

There is a clear sense that life is a countdown to death, whether long or short. We cannot even predict whether death will come to us “tomorrow”.

So, at some point I can no longer recall, I began writing things down deliberately, at a rhythm of my own, to record how things felt at the time. These words would string a life together into a long, private chronicle of personal relics, or a “biographical film”.

Someone unknown—probably very few people will care about, or read, my experiences and thoughts, including my descendants (if I have any, haha). Still, there is pleasure to be found in observing oneself.

I once imagined becoming a wandering ghost, an “invisible” observer, watching myself hurry from one end of the street to the other, with a posture and expression both familiar and strange, so distinctly “me”.

It feels like a pity that we cannot observe ourselves from the outside. Perhaps that is one of the reasons I began to write.
