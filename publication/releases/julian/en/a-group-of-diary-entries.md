*Written in Chinese by River. Translated into English by ChatGPT.*

## One

On the Monday of my third week at work, I attended a programming language training session. By the time I finished work, it was already half past eight in the evening. It was raining as I rode my electric bike home. It reminded me of sitting behind my mother on her electric bike when I was little. When it rained, I would shelter beneath her raincoat, and all I could see was its red fabric. Everything was damp, and my shoes were soaked through. I kept wondering when we would get home, so this uncomfortable feeling could end. Later, our family got a car, and those rainy rides gradually faded from my memory.

Now I am the one riding in front.

## Two

Coming home after a day at work is a happy moment, especially when I have not had to work overtime. My books lie scattered across the living room floor. I can pick up any one of them and start reading. Or lie lazily on my stomach on the rug, opening a book to the page where I left off. Listening to the music I love. ‘Past Lives’ is playing now. Tonight, I plan to finish watching *Someday We’ll Tell Each Other Everything*, which I left unfinished last night.

I do all I can to carve my life into a shape I love.
