# When Freedom Falls Silent

2026.01.05

*Written in Chinese by River. Translated into English by ChatGPT.*

An inescapable question: if I quit my job and left everything behind, what would I most want to do? Even without having to earn a living, could I really spend my parents' money as I pleased, supposedly “travelling the world”?

My greatest wish is—for time to be used to create meaning. Yet time does not seem to be meaning's only raw material. Before that, I must find an “anchor” to hold me—a place in society—instead of becoming a wandering ghost.

The shell I inhabit now seems unable to bear the weight of my heart. Yet finding a new one is so difficult. This is what troubles me most at present.

Many people make their homes within the promises civilisation offers them—they are its believers. But perhaps they simply have not yet been betrayed by it. Perhaps even after that betrayal, they are unwilling to leave. They have made civilisation their home and choose to ignore the signs that it is beginning to crumble. Yes, this is a way of living. Though it may not work.

I have simply encountered this problem earlier—a vast avalanche. Life no longer promises anything. Beyond the moment in which it takes place, no action seems to guarantee an “outcome” worth looking forward to. I try to understand the soul's cycle of rebirth, the movement of all things through cause and effect. But what troubles me now is not whether actions produce results, but whether those results answer our hopes. I can only imagine God's gaze upon “us”. Within our circumstances, we can only choose, choose, choose, living out our philosophy of life. There is nothing else.

And when I truly found myself in these circumstances, I felt an immense void. It takes so much effort to keep my life going, to keep it running in a way that looks “normal”. Only I know that every day I fight, then survive, then build a new self among the ruins of the day before—over and over again.
