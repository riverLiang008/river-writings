# Julian

2025.08.10

*Written in Chinese by River. Translated into English by ChatGPT.*

From *A Cloud* to *Julian*, I want to record in words the passage from youth into adulthood. Those inward, hidden changes can sometimes be hard even for me to recognise.

I believe that keeping a record is a form of resistance.

When I read Nanami Shiono’s history of the Romans, Julian’s story left a deep impression on me. He was someone who held ideals and tried to act on them, yet ultimately did not achieve what he hoped for. That is where this collection gets its name. I do not know how long the things a person cherishes can survive in the world, or what they might become.

I have left the ivory tower called ‘school’ and entered the maze of society. I once explored the world with passion, setting out on my adventures through travel. Now I need to face the reality before me.

I still want to travel, but what it means to me has changed. In the past, I could briefly leave my familiar life behind and go to see landscapes that had nothing to do with me. But wherever I go, I must eventually return and face my own life.

I hope that what I am about to write will come from a desire to explore, rather than from nostalgia. How long can my resistance last, and how far can it go? I do not know. I want to write these changes down.

Li Bai wrote, ‘A day will come to ride the wind and break the waves; I will hoist my sail to the clouds and cross the vast sea.’<sup id="note-ref-1"><a href="#note-1">1</a></sup> I envy his optimism. Unfortunately, I am not a particularly optimistic person by nature. But I still want to take my sincerity with me and leap into the sea of my own life.
