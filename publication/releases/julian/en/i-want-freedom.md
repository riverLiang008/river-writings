# I Want Freedom

2026.01.07

*Written in Chinese by River. Translated into English by ChatGPT.*

I want freedom. But I do not know what freedom means.

In my world, love and hate are both intense. There is no middle ground. I cannot bring myself to let others in, knowing that it would mean pain for them too. If someone has known this kind of pain, perhaps he is the person I am looking for.

Borges's words, “the hunger of my heart”,<sup id="note-ref-1"><a href="#note-1">1</a></sup> keep echoing in my mind. Yes, my heart hungers. I cannot be content with what I have. My heart longs for freedom, for deep breaths, for touching the grass, for snow-covered mountains and open wilderness. Alone.

In this cold and solitude that could almost sweep a soul into the abyss, I find a moment's peace. Peace—a Pure Land.

A wisp of my ghost still wanders in the Himalayas. Wanders. I roam aimlessly between earth and sky. The world is vast. I have nowhere to rest, yet everywhere is home.<sup id="note-ref-2"><a href="#note-2">2</a></sup>
