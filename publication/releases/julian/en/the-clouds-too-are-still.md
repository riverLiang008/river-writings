# The Clouds, Too, Are Still

2025.12.12

*Written in Chinese by River. Translated into English by ChatGPT.*

Fallen to the bottom of a very, very deep valley. Not a breath of wind. The clouds, too, are still. I lie in a heap of grass, my body sinking into it, watching the motionless world.

All along, I have approached the world as a visitor, stubbornly demanding an answer to its riddle, an explanation, a clear truth. Yet He appears and disappears within my fate, often making a carefree exit with a Cheshire Cat smile, as in *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*, leaving only the hollow echo of my voice.

I ask Him: why do I exist?

He answers: you have existed since long, long ago, as far back as the birth of this universe.

I ask Him: where am I going?

He answers: you will go back to where you came from.

I do not understand. He smiles.

All along, I have been a favoured child of heaven, light, heat, the sun. I have taken everything I have for granted and kept as much distance as possible from those around me. But when I have to face pain far beyond what I can bear, I turn to Him again and plead: please let me go on living happily, lightly. I am in too much pain. I can feed only on fantasies and dreams. I spend more time dreaming than ever before—imagining my future self looking tenderly at who I am now. Or please, let me close my eyes and have this time pass.

All along, I have felt that my life must be full—time must not be wasted, nor flowers, spring or the moon. But at some point, midnight fell, and pain took hold of me. I steep myself in my pain, polishing it over and over, while I gradually become a string pulled taut to its limit, on the verge of snapping.

Then, with the clear snap of a breaking string, I sink to the bottom of the valley. Back to where the story began.

“At the furthest edge of despair, can you too feel a strange kind of freedom?”
