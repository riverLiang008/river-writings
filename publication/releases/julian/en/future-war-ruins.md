# The War Ruins of the Future

2025.12.06

*Written in Chinese by River. Translated into English by ChatGPT.*

I collapse onto the living-room floor like a puppet fallen apart. I have become a form of pain. Like termites eating through a wooden house, the company, that enormous beast, is eating away at my heart. Late each night, I return to the space I have made for myself and secretly mend my heart. I need sleep, quiet and exile. Exile into those elusive dreams of mine, barely there.

Four months now. Pain has different layers, mediums and textures, but its essence remains unchanged. I am tormented. I pray so desperately for a storm to pass, for a brief respite. Yet when I quietly examine my life as an outsider, I realise that in this lightless hell, the soul is letting out a silent cry. An absurd game of magical realism, taken seriously by everyone: this is our reality.

I care only about my own pain. An impoverished artist out of Walser,<sup id="note-ref-1"><a href="#note-1">1</a></sup> a soul mired in mud, humbly begging from the world. In despair, I discover that pain is not a form of life but the substance of fate. The road to tragedy is crowded. In our lives, we die more than once.

I have prepared one last escape route for myself—quitting next March, even without another job lined up. Six months ago, I could never have imagined coming to this point. And my future self will return again and again to commemorate who I am now, as later generations visit the ruins of a war: building a memorial somewhere in the shattered city, while elsewhere raising new buildings.
